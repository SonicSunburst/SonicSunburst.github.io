SONIC SUNBURST

Sonic Sunburst is a classic-style Sonic fan game set on Little Planet. Eggman is constructing a Dyson sphere around the Sun, and Sonic has only minutes to stop the activation sequence before the entire Solar System is thrown into permanent eclipse.

The adventure is built around speed, dangerous platforming, time travel, and a stronger emphasis on the Insta-Shield. Sonic can move between the Present and the Past to sabotage Eggman's machinery, create more time on the Doomsday Clock, and push deeper into Little Planet.
